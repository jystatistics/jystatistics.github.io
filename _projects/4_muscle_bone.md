Muscle and bone age together. This line of work asks how muscle decline changes fracture risk and how to model the two jointly.

- Role of sarcopenia and muscle decline in fracture risk
- Joint modeling of longitudinal bone and muscle measures
- Shared genetic architecture between muscle and bone
- Handgrip-defined sarcopenia and polygenic risk for 10-year fracture in Black, Hispanic, and White women
