People age at different speeds, and clinical visits happen at irregular times. This project builds models that follow each person's aging path over time.

- **PoA-HyTrax / SS-HyTrax:** self-supervised, time-aware transformers using masked trajectory modeling and delta-time tokens to model non-linear biological aging across irregular visits
- **Multi-omic fusion:** combining longitudinal epigenetic and proteomic data with clinical phenotypes into sensitive aging scores
- **Validation:** testing whether these scores detect slowed aging in randomized trials (CALERIE, FAST Initiative) and harmonized multi-cohort biobank data
- **Equity:** checking calibration and bias across racial, socioeconomic, and underrepresented groups

Supported by an NIA Biomarker Network pilot project (2026–2027), with Dr. Daniel Belsky at the Columbia Aging Center.
