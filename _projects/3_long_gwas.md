Most genetic studies of bone use a single BMD measurement. This work models how genetic effects change over time.

- Time-varying effect models for BMD trajectories
- Multi-ancestry analysis across WHI, SOF, MrOS, and FHS
- Identified SPTBN1 as a key regulator of BMD trajectories
- Polygenic risk scores for major osteoporotic fracture
