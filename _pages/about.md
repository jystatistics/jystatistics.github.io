I am a Postdoctoral Research Scientist at the Robert N. Butler Columbia Aging Center, Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health, working with Dr. Daniel Belsky. I build AI and statistical methods that turn repeated clinical, genomic, and multi-omic measurements into **trajectories of aging**, so that we can spot people at rising risk early and measure whether interventions slow biological aging.

**Research interests**

- **AI-driven fracture prediction and musculoskeletal aging.** Deep sequential models (HyTrax) that combine polygenic risk with serial bone density, grip strength, and lean mass to predict fracture beyond FRAX.
- **Deep sequence modeling of biological aging.** Time-aware transformer architectures (PoA-HyTrax) for irregular clinical visits and non-linear aging trajectories.
- **Multi-omic data fusion in geroscience.** Integrating longitudinal epigenetic and proteomic data with clinical phenotypes into sensitive aging scores.
- **Biomarker validation.** Testing aging measures in randomized trials (CALERIE, FAST Initiative) and large biobanks.
- **Algorithmic stewardship and health equity.** Auditing clinical risk scores and aging AI tools for calibration drift and bias across diverse populations.
- **NLP for clinical phenotyping.** Using large language models to extract frailty, functional status, and cognitive decline from clinical notes.

I received my PhD in Biomedical Science (Biomedical Informatics) from The Ohio State University in 2026, advised by Dr. Qing Wu. Before that I earned an MS in Mathematics and Statistics at Minnesota State University, Mankato, and worked as a data analyst at IMS Health in Seoul.
